PHQ-9 PATIENT HEALTH QUESTIONNAIRE - CLINICAL GUIDE

OVERVIEW
The PHQ-9 is a validated 9-item depression screening tool used in clinical practice.
Each item is scored 0 (Not at all) to 3 (Nearly every day). Maximum score is 27.
It is used to screen for depression, monitor severity, and track treatment response.

THE 9 QUESTIONS
1. Little interest or pleasure in doing things
2. Feeling down, depressed, or hopeless
3. Trouble falling or staying asleep, or sleeping too much
4. Feeling tired or having little energy
5. Poor appetite or overeating
6. Feeling bad about yourself or that you are a failure
7. Trouble concentrating on things
8. Moving or speaking so slowly or being fidgety or restless
9. Thoughts that you would be better off dead or of hurting yourself

SCORING INTERPRETATION
Score 0-4: Minimal depression. Monitor patient, repeat PHQ-9 as clinically indicated. No treatment required unless patient reports significant distress.
Score 5-9: Mild depression. Watchful waiting, repeat PHQ-9 at follow-up in 2-4 weeks. Consider counseling and psychoeducation.
Score 10-14: Moderate depression. Create a treatment plan, consider counseling and pharmacotherapy evaluation. Repeat in 2 weeks.
Score 15-19: Moderately severe depression. Active treatment with pharmacotherapy and psychotherapy is recommended. Repeat in 1 week.
Score 20-27: Severe depression. Immediate initiation of pharmacotherapy and expedited referral to mental health specialist. Consider hospitalization if safety is a concern.

ITEM 9 - SUICIDE RISK PROTOCOL
Item 9 asks about thoughts of death or self-harm. Any response above 0 on Item 9 requires:
1. Immediate clinical assessment of suicide risk using Columbia Protocol
2. Documentation of safety planning conversation
3. Consideration of crisis intervention services
4. Possible emergency referral if risk is imminent
5. Notify supervisor or senior clinician immediately
6. Do not leave patient alone if risk is high

CLINICALLY SIGNIFICANT IMPROVEMENT
A reduction of 5 points from baseline is considered clinically significant improvement.
Full remission is defined as a PHQ-9 score below 5.
Treatment response is defined as 50% or greater reduction from baseline score.

FOLLOW-UP TIMING BY SEVERITY
Minimal (0-4): Repeat in 6 months
Mild (5-9): Repeat in 2-4 weeks
Moderate (10-14): Repeat in 2 weeks
Moderately Severe (15-19): Repeat in 1 week
Severe (20-27): Immediate follow-up, daily monitoring recommended

TREATMENT ESCALATION TRIGGERS
- PHQ-9 score increases by 5 or more points from baseline
- Any positive response to Item 9 (suicide risk item)
- Patient reports inability to function at work, home, or social settings
- Patient expresses hopelessness or helplessness consistently
- No improvement after 4-6 weeks of current treatment
- Patient requests more intensive treatment
